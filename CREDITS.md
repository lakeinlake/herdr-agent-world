Pokémon artwork belongs to its respective rights holders, including The Pokémon
Company, Nintendo and Game Freak. These are original game graphics, not CC0
artwork. Herdr Agent World is an independent project and is not affiliated with
or endorsed by those rights holders.

## Animated characters

The 30 bundled characters use the regular-color, front-facing Pokémon Black &
White animations. Pikachu comes from [Pokémon Database](https://img.pokemondb.net/sprites/black-white/anim/normal/pikachu.gif).
The remaining characters come from [PokeAPI/sprites](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites/tree/a3a1432e688ea028f12c51371d5253037cb9f17b/sprites/pokemon/versions/generation-v/black-white/animated),
revision `a3a1432e688ea028f12c51371d5253037cb9f17b`. See that repository's
[image ownership notice](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites/blob/a3a1432e688ea028f12c51371d5253037cb9f17b/LICENCE.txt).
Animations are converted to local PNG sprite sheets with consistent frame sizing.
Static character thumbnails are used for defaults and some decorations.

## Scenery

- Pokémon Emerald exterior tilesheet: [The Spriters Resource, asset 61816](https://www.spriters-resource.com/game_boy_advance/pokemonemerald/asset/61816/),
  ripped by Heartlessdragoon. [Source site terms](https://www.spriters-resource.com/page/tou/).
- FireRed trainers, Pokémon, props, buildings and landscape graphics:
  [pret/pokefirered](https://github.com/pret/pokefirered/tree/037335f4c725d7c9aecdac87066f2002b4bd7e14),
  revision `037335f4c725d7c9aecdac87066f2002b4bd7e14`. Buildings are assembled
  from original map, tile and palette data.
- Emerald female Swimmer: [Bulbagarden Archives](https://archives.bulbagarden.net/media/upload/7/77/Spr_RS_Swimmer_F.png),
  also identified in [pret/pokeemerald](https://github.com/pret/pokeemerald/blob/master/graphics/trainers/front_pics/swimmer_f.png).

All artwork is bundled in the executable and served locally. The monitor does
not fetch artwork at runtime. Software dependency licenses are collected in
[Third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
